# 7/12/2026 11 PM - Training the arm

_Time spent: 6h_

After everything was set up and ready, I began collecting data to train the ACT model for the arm. I recorded 134 demonstration episodes using two cameras while controlling the follower arm with the leader arm. During each episode, I tried to do the stamping action as cleanly and consistently as I could so the model would have good data to learn from.
Once the dataset was finished, I uploaded it to Lightning AI and started training the ACT policy using my free GPU credits. I selected the fastest GPU available, which was an NVIDIA H100, and set the training run to complete 30000 steps. Even with the H100, the model was still taking a while to train. I watched it for about an hour, but since it was already late and the training could continue without me, I went to sleep and left it running overnight.
When I woke up, I realized that my free Lightning AI credits had run out and the training had stopped at 20000 out of 30000 steps. Since most of the training was already complete, I decided to continue using the 20000-step checkpoint. I purchased $5 of additional credits so I could save the training progress and download the trained policy.
Lastly, I loaded the model onto the robotic arm and recorded several demos using the ACT model trained from the 134 episodes. The arm was able to complete the stamping action successfully, although it sometimes did not press the stamp button down hard enough.
