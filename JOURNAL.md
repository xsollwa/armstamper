# 7/10/2026 8 PM - Fixing the fails

_Time spent: 2h_

As I was assembling this project, I came across several issues with my initial design. First, the paper tray was too large to fit on my 3D printer’s build plate. Second, the press-fit electronics casing was too tight. Third, I found out that apparently PLA does not work well as a stamping material on paper. Based on these issues, I revised the CAD models for the paper tray and electronics casing, then ordered an actual rubber stamp and ink pad from Amazon.

# 6/5/2026 6 AM - Brainstorming and Design

_Time spent: 3h_

After participating in Prototype, I have received a Hugging Face LeRobot arm, which is commonly used for imitation learning. I wanted to gain hands-on experience training the arm to interact with additional hardware that I would design and build myself. 
My main challenge was deciding what type of hardware to make while keeping in mind the arm's capabilities and the limitations I saw at Prototype.

Ultimately, I decided to make a mini electronic paper stamper that the arm can interact with through stamping a paper and giving it back to the user. 

I sketched out the initial design for training setup and made the first draft of my BOM which I will enter into this project. I also researched potential platforms, like Kaggle, that provide GPU access and could be used to train the imitation learning model.
